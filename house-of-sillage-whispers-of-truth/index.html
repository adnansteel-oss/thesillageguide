At the heart of House of Sillage Whispers of Truth lies a beautifully orchestrated olfactive journey, mirroring the path of self-discovery and destiny. The Bloom of Grace (Middle Notes): As your personal journey unfolds, the fragrance transitions to its heart, where rose and jasmine bloom. Moss grounds the spirit, offering stability, while musk whispers of an alluring, deep presence.
